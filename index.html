What we shoot

Pick your game

Long Range target counters let trained members open and run any field themselves, any time during daylight hours.

01

Skeet

Classic high-house / low-house crossers across eight stations. Wireless voice remote available on field 2.

Self-serve
02

Trap

Going-away targets from the trap house. Tuesday and summer Wednesday night leagues.

Self-serve
03

Heated 5-Stand

Sporting-clays variety with a roof and heat. Shoot all winter long. Reserve online.

West Outdoor 5-Stand

Six throwers, voice or controller release. Practice solo or bring a group. We'll show you how.

Everyone is welcome

Shoot when it suits you

With our Long Range Target Counting system you can open a skeet, trap or 5-stand field any time during daylight hours. Stop in during clubhouse hours, meet the members, and sign up for a membership to get set up.

  • Members shoot 8:00 AM to dusk, 7 days a week
  • Bring a guest: $5 non-member daily use fee
  • Shells for sale: 12, 20, 28 ga & .410
  • Located right off I-41 on Green Bay's north side
